# References

Selected references from the thesis that are most useful for builders. The full list (52 entries) is in Chapter 8 of the thesis.

## Hydroponics

- H. M. Resh, *Hydroponic Food Production: A Definitive Guidebook for the Advanced Home Gardener and the Commercial Hydroponic Grower*. CRC Press, 2012.
- C. Sonneveld and W. Voogt, *Plant Nutrition of Greenhouse Crops*. Springer, 2009.
- D. Savvas and H. Passam, *Hydroponic Production of Vegetables and Ornamentals*. Embryo Publications, 2002.
- FAO, *Good Agricultural Practices for greenhouse vegetable production in the South East European countries*. Rome, 2017.
- FAO, *Water Quality for Agriculture*. Rome.

## Measurement and signal processing

- J. P. Bentley, *Principles of Measurement Systems*, 4th ed. Pearson, 2005.
- S. Haykin and B. Van Veen, *Signals and Systems*, 2nd ed. Wiley, 2002.

## Software and connectivity

- ESPHome documentation: https://esphome.io/
- Home Assistant documentation: https://www.home-assistant.io/docs/
- ZeroTier, *Getting Started*: https://docs.zerotier.com/start
- A. Banks and R. Gupta, *MQTT Version 3.1.1*, OASIS Standard, 2015.

## Design, standards and economics

- K. T. Ulrich and S. D. Eppinger, *Product Design and Development*, 6th ed. McGraw-Hill, 2016.
- ISO 9241-210, *Ergonomics of human-system interaction: Human-centred design for interactive systems*.
- IEC 60529, *Degrees of protection provided by enclosures (IP Code)*.
- R. A. Brealey, S. C. Myers and F. Allen, *Principles of Corporate Finance*, 12th ed. McGraw-Hill, 2017.
