# Client interview questions

The questions used to gather requirements from the farm (thesis Appendix 9.7, translated). Reuse them if you're adapting Chaac for another grower.

## Usability

1. How important is it that the interface is easy to use and understand?
2. Do you prefer a digital interface, or something simpler like physical buttons?
3. How important is remote access, e.g. from a phone or computer?
4. Would you like notifications on your phone when something unusual happens?
5. Would you like to control some functions manually, such as adjusting pH or EC doses?

## Space and maintenance

6. How important is it that the machine is small and easy to move?
7. How much space is available for the system?
8. How important is it that spare parts are easy to get and inexpensive?
9. Would you do basic maintenance yourself, or prefer specialized support?

## Integration and growth

10. Should the system work with other devices on your network (environmental monitoring, cameras, irrigation)?
11. Should it integrate with your current automation platform (Apple Home or similar)?
12. Should it be scalable, so you can add modules, sensors or features later?
13. How important are easy software updates?

## Security and reliability

14. What are your security expectations? Should access be protected by passwords or other mechanisms?
15. How concerned are you about failures? How important is a data backup?

## Budget and environment

16. What is your approximate budget for buying and installing the system?
17. Do you prefer a cheaper solution with fewer features, or a larger investment in more automation and accuracy?
18. Is low energy use or low environmental impact important?
19. What is the technical background of the operators?
20. What are the environmental conditions in the facility?
21. What internet connectivity is available on site?
